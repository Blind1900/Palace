Construction growth lab

A separate construction experiment begins with three free foundation members. Choose a frame height and restart construction to apply it; the current frame is discarded. Vertical members are thicker than horizontal ones at every height. Taller frames have more members and cost more HM; the eleven-row frame uses thicker, stronger vertical members. The evenly spaced dotted blueprint grows from the foundation and branches at connected joints over four simulated seconds. Then, once per simulated second, all eligible connected branches can place or repair members and bonds in parallel, each using the existing repair rules and costing HM. A newly placed member can support further work on the next attempt. Completed members use the existing structural simulation.

Remove ground to interrupt support; clear loose members and restore ground before rebuilding. Other eligible sites can still be worked on. The status reports how many members or bonds were completed, HM spent and particle or foundation obstacles detected on the last attempt, even if other sites progressed. Building overlap and missing connections can also block work without appearing in that list. Completion stops automatic work. This lab has no save slot and does not change the live game or other lab saves. It does not model workers or SM.

Breach the basin to send water toward the frame. Optional water damage weakens attached members based on water depth; yellow and red show damage. Water overlapping a repair site blocks work there; water beside a clear site can still cause damage and repeated paid repairs. Upright members use a coarse centerline blocker and a visual contact fill, so particle cells can overlap visible edges. Blast the lower left anchor to test damage during construction. These are experimental grid rules, not physical pressure or live-game behavior. Restart turns water damage off.
