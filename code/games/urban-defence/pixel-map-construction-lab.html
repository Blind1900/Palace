Inside-mode deliveries originate at the center pixel of each 3-by-3 buried core, costing one HM each. Overlapping deliveries can share that center. If it is destroyed, another surviving pixel in the same core supplies deliveries; a fully destroyed base stops emitting until repaired. Connecting floors are one-cell-thick horizontal sections directly below the deeper neighboring core, with higher pillars extended down to meet them. Floors/extensions are free initial lab foundation material, not additional cores. Terrain below stays intact; water, tunnels or other material in a proposed floor reject that site rather than being overwritten. Basement excavation reaches each level floor without flattening the surrounding ground.

Buried-core mode extends each three-cell-wide pillar three cells into the ground: 27 additional free core pixels plus the original nine seeds. Without connecting floors, the basement follows the local surface three cells down; with floors, excavation reaches the level floor sections. Water and other occupied material are never removed. Gold marks surviving buried core pixels. Any surviving core pixel permits paid repair. Losing all 27 permanently disables construction and repair; terrain restoration cannot revive the building. Existing travelling material remains accounted for but stops repairing. These placement earthworks are free lab fixtures, not live gameplay costs.

Inside mode carries paid pixels in a separate travelling layer: they crawl cardinally over settled beams/supports and ground access. They choose the nearest reachable available repair target using shortest routes and can overlap other deliveries immediately, without traffic detours or yielding. Pale squares are deliveries; white means multiple deliveries at that position. Terrain interiors, water and debris cannot be traversed. Targets remain unique, and a repair becomes solid only in an empty, supported cell. Overlap does not merge material or charge/refund HM. Blasts also destroy travelling pixels in their radius. Unsupported or flooded deliveries wait; no teleporting. This mode is lab-only.

Floor excavation removes only three terrain cells beneath the span midpoint. It never removes floor/core material, water or debris, and gives no HM refund. Support and weight react on the next physics tick; remaining ground may still hold the floor.

Restoration refills only originally imported terrain that is now empty. Water, debris and building material are never overwritten; lab restoration costs no HM. Clearing debris gives no refund.
