Inspect a separate particle snapshot of a newly generated game map, or the synthetic diagonal-tunnel test. The green line marks the surface, cyan is water, and gold outlines underground openings; solid terrain is black, as in the particle world lab. Click to blast terrain or switch to Inspect passage. Scroll and zoom to see details.

Inspect passage highlights a local cross-section. Route test checks a four-neighbor path between two clicked cells through original imported openings, rejecting flooded or obstructed cross-sections. It is an approximation, not miner pathfinding: unit size, newly excavated passages and live gameplay are not modeled. Running or editing clears the route. Buildings, units and game saves are not imported.
