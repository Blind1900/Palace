Building collapse lab

Click the building to blast it. Taller frames have thicker, tougher vertical walls throughout the building, rather than reinforced base blocks. Their thicker walls also have greater load capacity and use their visible width for collisions. Damaged supports can fail under the weight above them, causing a progressive collapse. Supported debris slows from friction but can still tip or be scattered by a blast. This is a gameplay approximation, not an engineering simulation. Sloped sites use a level frame with foundation piers; changing the ground profile resets the lab. Surviving frames rebuild using HM; work pauses if the site is blocked or HM runs out. Fully destroyed buildings cannot rebuild. Move with A/D or left/right arrows; use W/S or up/down arrows at the ladder to climb. Falling debris can injure the trooper.
