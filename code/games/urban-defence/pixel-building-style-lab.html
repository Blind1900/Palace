Building type, shape & style lab

Pick a building type: columns are its shape variants and rows are the three faction styles (Asian, Gothic, modern). Every card is its own particle world, so the preview is real structure under load. Floors are spaced so a trooper fits with step headroom, every storey holds a few soldiers (more at higher levels) and one-way switchback stairs let units walk up through the building. Military buildings spawn units from the hangar, which houses the primary core. The Defences sheet shows the turrets, missile launchers, interceptors, control panels and guard troops. On the Garrison sheet guard troops man the side windows of population buildings and shoot out at red attackers, who return fire; walls and roofs block shots and only window glass lets them through.

The District sheet tests how buildings fit together on a real street instead of alone. Columns are layouts: Wide plaza (three towers with wide gaps for street furniture and pocket parks), Military merge (a military compound squeezed between two population towers), Tight block (four population towers with 5, 12 and 28 px gaps) and Military base (a military compound beside a population tower and a farm). Rows are each style, plus Mixed, where neighbours have different styles. Each card shows the infill between neighbours (skybridges across narrow gaps, street lamps, landmarks, or a park tree where two styles meet), the paved road along the building bases, and the hangar runway. The text under each card reports bridges and props per gap, blocked doors or runway pixels, whether a mech fits the hangar, and the structural load. Unit demo sends a trooper walking down the whole street through every ground floor, plus hangar traffic.

The Antenna variants sheet puts every rooftop crown on every population shape (columns: slab to needle; rows: style and crown), and each card reports how many crown pixels were built, flagging CLIPPED crowns. The crowns are: Asian dragon, dragon pillar and twin dragons around a sorin; Gothic angel spire, belfry and triple spires; modern broadcast mast, stepped needle and dish deck. Elsewhere each building picks its crown from a stable hash of its type, level, shape and roof position, so a street mixes them.
